In the previous dotprod example, what fraction of the total references to x and y
will be hits once we have padded array x?

float dotprod(float x[12], float y[8])
{
    float sum = 0.0;
    int i;

    for (i = 0; i < 8; i++)
        sum += x[i] * y[i];
    return sum;
}

4 misses - x[0], y[0], x[4], y[4]
16 - 4 = 12 hits

4 / 16 * 100 = 25% misses
100 - 25 = 75% hits
